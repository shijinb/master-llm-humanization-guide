# Review checklist

Run before publishing. Every box must be ticked or the exception written down.

## Accuracy (gate)
- [ ] Every factual claim is on the claims list with a source I have opened
- [ ] No `[NEEDS:` or `[UNVERIFIED:` markers remain
- [ ] Links open and point to what they claim
- [ ] Prices, limits and versions checked today against official pages
- [ ] Quotes and customer names have permission

## Honesty (gate)
- [ ] No invented experience, testimonial, result or persona
- [ ] Limitations and trade-offs stated where they matter
- [ ] AI-assistance disclosure handled where required

## Reader value
- [ ] Opens with the point, not a definition or warm-up
- [ ] Every major section adds something not on the first page of search results
- [ ] Main points made once; repeats add something new
- [ ] Ends with an action, decision rule or honest open question

## Style
- [ ] `prose-check` warnings cleared or deliberately kept
- [ ] Read aloud; no sentence needs two breaths
- [ ] Repeated blocks (cards, FAQs) vary in shape
- [ ] Tone matches the audience preset

## Variants and publishing
- [ ] Swap test: no section would fit another variant unchanged
- [ ] Tracking parameters, IDs, canonical URL and structured data checked separately
- [ ] Target record confirmed by two identifiers before writing to the live system
- [ ] Compared section by section with the live source after publishing
- [ ] Unrelated issues reported to their owner, not fixed in this change
