# Prompt: business email

```text
TO: {role, relationship}
GOAL: {the one thing you need from them}, by {date}
FACTS: {context they need, with numbers}
TONE: {preset}

RULES
- Subject line: topic + ask, under 60 characters, no exclamation marks.
- First sentence states the point or the ask.
- Under 150 words. One request.
- No "I hope this email finds you well", "just checking in", "per my last email".
- Do not add facts, dates or commitments that are not in FACTS.

OUTPUT
Subject line, then the email.
```
