# Changelog

Semantic versioning; see [README § Versioning](README.md#versioning).

## [1.0.0] – 2026-10-25

First public version.

- Guide: nine chapters covering principles, style, formats (including technical docs, resumes and email), prompt design, accuracy and human review, editing, evaluation, ethics and production lessons.
- Templates: new content, content updates, variant pages, technical docs, resume bullets, email, facts sheet, review checklist.
- Evaluation: eight-criterion rubric (`eval/rubric.json`) with accuracy and honesty gates; four test cases.
- `prose-check` 1.0.0: seven rules, text and JSON output, `--fail-on` for CI.

Derived from an internal content guide used in production. Company-specific material (product claims, market data, named staff, case studies) was removed, examples were rewritten as synthetic ones, and guidance aimed at passing AI detectors was replaced with an explanation of why that is not a goal.
